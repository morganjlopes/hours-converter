Hours Converter

Convert spreadsheet time values between H:MM and decimal hours.

How the conversion works
159:21 ↔ 159.35
183:39 ↔ 183.65
78:21 ↔ 78.35
115:44 ↔ 115.73

H:MM → Decimal: cells matching HOURS:MINUTES become hours + minutes/60, rounded to two decimals.

Decimal → H:MM: only numeric cells that look like decimal hours (formatted as 0.00 or with a fractional part) are converted, so columns like "Worked days" or "Pay" stay intact.

Names, phone numbers, dates, and other values pass through untouched.

Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded.
